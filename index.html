Frequently asked questions

Is RenovateIQ free? +
RenovateIQ is free to download with no account or login required. The free version includes 1 project, task checklists, budget tracking, photo gallery and weekly notifications. How do I plan a home renovation? +
Start by defining your scope and setting a realistic budget — including a 15-20% contingency for unexpected costs. Break the project into phases: preparation, build and finishing. Get at least three quotes from contractors, agree a payment schedule and track every expense as you go.
How do I track my renovation budget? +
Set your total budget before work begins, then log every expense as it happens — contractor invoices, materials, fixtures and delivery charges. RenovateIQ's budget tracker categorises your spending so you can see exactly where the money is going.
How do I find reliable tradespeople? +
Always get a minimum of three quotes and ask for references from recent similar projects. Check that tradespeople are registered with the relevant bodies — Gas Safe for gas work, NICEIC or NAPIT for electrical work. Review their ratings on Checkatrade or Trustpilot and never pay the full amount upfront.
